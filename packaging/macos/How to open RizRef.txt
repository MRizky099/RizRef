RizRef for macOS — first launch
================================

1. Drag RizRef onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open it once with the steps for your macOS version. After that it opens
   normally, from Launchpad, the Dock or a double-clicked .rizref board.

   RizRef is free and open source, and it is not signed with a paid Apple
   Developer ID, so macOS cannot check who made it and asks you to confirm
   the first time. Nothing is changed on your Mac apart from allowing
   RizRef.

   macOS 15 Sequoia and later:
     - Double-click RizRef. macOS says it "cannot verify" the app: click Done.
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to
       "RizRef was blocked", click Open Anyway and confirm.

   macOS 10.13 High Sierra to macOS 14 Sonoma:
     - Control-click (or right-click) RizRef in Applications, choose Open,
       then click Open in the dialog.

   Or, in Terminal (any version):
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/RizRef.app

Requirements: macOS 10.13 or later on Intel, macOS 11 or later on Apple
Silicon. One app runs natively on both.

Source code, checksums and issues: https://github.com/MRizky099/RizRef
